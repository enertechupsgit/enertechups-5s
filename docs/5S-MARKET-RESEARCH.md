# 5S software market research and product implications

Research date: 23 October 2026; synthesis reviewed 24 October 2026. Purpose: inform the Enertech web/mobile master plan. Scope: Google discovery, public vendor product pages, vendor documentation and relevant technology documentation. No paid trials, sales submissions, account creation or hands-on paid-product testing were performed.

This is a broad representative survey, not a claim to have exhaustively examined every 5S product worldwide. The category includes dedicated 5S tools, inspection platforms, manufacturing execution tools, CMMS products and broader EHS/daily-management suites. An unmentioned feature is unknown, not necessarily absent. Pricing, plan entitlements and exact offline behavior require current vendor confirmation; no ranking is asserted.

## 2. User references and how they were used

### MaintainX image

The supplied `MaintainX Application UI.png` shows desktop list/detail assets, search/filter controls, related work, sub-assets, responsible teams and a mobile asset detail view. It also illustrates status history and uptime/planned/unplanned downtime cards.

Implications: build a responsive information architecture; preserve domain relationships in the database; design native mobile navigation separately; calculate reliability only from actual time-stamped events and stated reporting windows. Use Enertech branding and our own components. No UI implementation or generated mockup was produced during this research.

Read the referenced [Weever comparison of ten 5S tools](https://weeversoftware.com/blog/5s-audit-app), dated 11 August 2026. It covers Weever, EASE, SafetyCulture, Lumiform, GoAudits, Tulip, Alpha TransForm, Inspectly360, Tervene and Quentic. Core product observations above use the relevant vendor's own material where available.

## 3. Patterns that should become our baseline

These are our synthesis of the reviewed sources, not a claim that every vendor offers every item:

1. A usable audit system stores answers, evidence and findings, not only five totals.
2. Every meaningful finding needs an accountable owner, due date and verified closure.
3. Offline capability must state cache preparation, supported actions, sync/error handling and access limits.
4. WDGLL reference images and published standard versions make scoring less subjective.
5. Scheduling and reminders support Sustain; missed checks must remain measurable.
6. Multi-site reports need consistent scoring definitions and permission-filtered data.
7. Web supports administration and comparison; native mobile supports camera, QR and on-floor execution.
8. Structured roles, object-level access, private evidence and durable history are foundational product behavior.
9. Red tagging needs disposition execution/verification, beyond taking a photo.
10. Analytics must expose late actions, repeated issues and stale audit coverage, alongside headline scores.

## 4. Suggested differentiation for Enertech

Focus on an integrated 5S workflow tailored to our actual plant structure: scan a zone/asset → inspect against its standard → raise a red tag or corrective action → assign the zonal leader → capture before/after → independent supervisor verification → observe the next audit's result.

Additional differentiators to validate during pilot: low typing burden, meaningful shared-device controls, English plus locally reviewed terminology, clear offline status, simple committee reviews and a verified benefits ledger. These are proposed product choices, not proven market advantages.

Avoid using feature count as the success metric. Prefer completion of scheduled checks, shorter overdue-action age, fewer repeat findings, reliable sync and operator adoption. AI assistance follows trustworthy data and measurable workflow value.

## 5. Technical evidence supporting the architecture proposal

| Decision | Official source | Interpretation for our plan |
|---|---|---|
| Native React approach | [React Native framework recommendation](https://reactnative.dev/blog/2024/06/25/use-a-framework-to-build-react-native-apps) | Expo is an appropriate candidate given the existing React/TypeScript team; perform real-device technical spikes before locking scope. |
| Local mobile persistence | [Expo SQLite](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/sqlite/), [local-first guide](https://docs.expo.dev/guides/local-first/) | SQLite provides persistence; domain-specific outbox, sync and conflict rules remain our responsibility. |
| Mobile secrets and notifications | [Expo SecureStore](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/securestore/), [notifications](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/notifications/) | Secure tokens separately from cached operational data; test push on production-capable builds and devices. |
| Identity integration | [Supabase JWT documentation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/jwts) | Verify signed provider tokens correctly; membership and business permissions stay in FastAPI. |
| Private media | [Supabase storage access controls](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/storage/security/access-control) | Private buckets/policies and short-lived authorized access; privileged storage credentials remain server-side. |
| Permission enforcement | [OWASP authorization guidance](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Authorization_Cheat_Sheet.html) | Deny by default, least privilege and checks on every request, including object access. |
| Tenant defense in database | [PostgreSQL row security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html) | RLS requires deliberate policies and a non-bypassing runtime role; test it, rather than assume enabling a flag guarantees isolation. |
| Recoverability | [PostgreSQL continuous archiving/PITR](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/continuous-archiving.html) | Managed PITR plus restore drills; image storage needs its own recovery policy. |

## 6. What still needs validation

- Actual Enertech forms, question weights, expected evidence and approval authority.
- Number of plants, zones, shifts, named users, shared phones and typical media volume.
- Existing identity provider, hosting/data-region requirements and device-management policy.
- Android/iOS versions, camera behavior, offline shift duration and shared-device restrictions.
- Retention, record export, privacy and contractual requirements, reviewed by appropriate company owners.
- Provider pricing and plan-specific SSO/PITR/backup features through current official quotes.
- Current vendor paid-product experience if a later buy-versus-build procurement decision is requested.

No claim of legal compliance, certification, guaranteed savings or production readiness is made by this research. The master plan defines the proposed work and measurable release gates; implementation awaits owner approval.
